Ticket rules

How buying, holding, sending and refunding tickets works on Marquee. Prices are in euros.

Finding events

  • Events lists upcoming events only: once an event has taken place it leaves the list and its tickets come off sale.
  • Search looks in event names, performers and venues. Date filters include both the first and the last day you pick.
  • "From" is the cheapest ticket you can buy right now. Sorting by price orders events by that "from" price.
  • "Few left" means 10 or fewer tickets remain. "Sold out" means every ticket type has gone.
  • Dates and times are shown in the venue's local time, with its offset from UTC.

Buying tickets

  • You can buy up to 8 tickets per order. Some events also have a limit per account, which counts every ticket you've bought for that event across all your orders.
  • Tickets in your basket are held for 10 minutes. When a hold runs out the tickets go back on sale and leave your basket.
  • Your basket holds tickets for one event at a time.
  • Early bird prices are available until the end of the date shown (venue time), while they last.
  • Concession tickets are for people under 16 or aged 65 and over on the day of the event. Add them one at a time with the holder's date of birth.
  • During a presale you need the presale code to buy tickets; general sale opens on the date shown.

Seats

  • At seated venues you choose your seats on the seat map. Seats in the Premium rows cost the Premium price.
  • Wheelchair spaces (♿) are kept for people who need them: tick "I need a wheelchair space" to choose one.
  • Don't leave a single empty seat: your seats can't leave one empty seat between them and a taken seat or the end of the row, on either side.

Prices and fees

  • Each ticket has a booking fee of 12% of its price, rounded to the nearest cent.
  • Each order also has one €2.50 order fee, however many tickets are in it.
  • The total in your basket is the total you pay at checkout.
  • Promo codes take a percentage off the ticket prices only, not the fees. One code per order: a new code replaces the one already applied. Codes can't be used after their end date.
  • Cards can be used until the end of the expiry month printed on them.

Your tickets

  • Your tickets and bookings are private: only you can open them.
  • Send a ticket to a friend with their name and email address, up to 2 days before the event. You can't send a ticket to yourself. Once sent, the ticket and its QR code belong to your friend, and your copy stops working.
  • You can resell a ticket on Marquee Resale for no more than its face value (the ticket price, without fees), up to 2 days before the event.
  • The face value on your ticket is the price you paid; later price changes don't affect tickets already sold.

Refunds

  • 15 days or more before the event: the full ticket price back.
  • 8 to 14 days before: half the ticket price back.
  • 7 days or fewer before: no refund.
  • Refunds are worked out from what you paid for the tickets, after any promo code. Booking and order fees aren't refunded.
  • If an event is postponed you can get a full refund, fees included, any time before the new date.
  • If an organiser cancels an event, everyone gets everything back automatically, fees included.

Waitlists

  • When every ticket type for an event has sold out you can join its waitlist. Your place is counted among the people waiting for that event.
  • If more tickets are released, people on the waitlist are offered them first, in the order they joined, before they go on general sale.

For organisers

  • Organisers see and manage only the events they run.
  • You can release more tickets up to the venue's capacity.
  • Ticket revenue is what fans paid for tickets on Marquee, after promo codes, not counting booking fees.
  • Each ticket can be checked in once; a second scan is refused as already checked in. Refunded, cancelled and transferred tickets are refused.
  • The attendee list download opens in any spreadsheet, one column per field.
